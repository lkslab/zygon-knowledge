## Additionality and 24/7 temporal matching (RFNBO / carbon-free-energy procurement)

- **card**: [additionality-and-24-7-matching](v1/additionality-and-24-7-matching.yaml) (5 papers)
- **primitive**: `pypsa/optimization/global_constraints.py:define_operational_limit` (pypsa 0.30.3)
- **exposed by**: neither, for this purpose — both models write the matching constraint by hand
- **reachable via**: Adding a GlobalConstraint row with type `operational_limit`, which the library then turns into one constraint over weighted dispatch of ONE carrier across the whole snapshot set, against a CONSTANT rhs. That is the nearest primitive and it is not sufficient: it is single-carrier, whole-horizon, and its rhs cannot contain decision variables, so it cannot express `generation >= x * consumption` and cannot be grouped by window. Annual matching against a fixed demand forecast is the one variant it can approximate, by pre-computing the demand as the constant.
