Subsidies, CfDs and PPAs settled on one wind farm

The 20 % cap year of the cost-recovery model is solved once; nothing about the dispatch changes. The wind farm's hourly output and the hourly price are then settled under an energy-only market, a two-sided contract for difference, a fixed premium, a cap-and-floor, a pay-as-produced PPA and a baseload PPA, plus a CfD variant that pays no top-up in zero-price hours. Same electrons, very different cheques.

Annual wind revenue by scheme
Same output, same prices. The CfD and the pay-as-produced PPA remove price risk; the premium and the floor add to it; the baseload PPA carries shape risk because the farm must buy back the flat volume in still hours.
Monthly revenue: market, CfD, premium
The market revenue follows the wind and the price; the CfD follows the wind only, so windy months with low prices are where the top-up is largest.
Price, strike and CfD top-up in one February week
The top-up is strike minus price per MWh produced: positive in cheap hours, negative (paid back) in the scarcity hours above the strike.
