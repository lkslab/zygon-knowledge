What a Generator is

One bus, one load, two Generators built from the same component: a wind farm whose availability changes every hour (`p_max_pu` a time series, `marginal_cost` 0) and a gas plant that is always available but costs 55 EUR/MWh to run. Each Generator is one decision per hour, bounded by its capacity times its availability, and the optimiser fills the load with the cheaper one first.

Two Generators on one bus
One component, two sets of attribute values: where it sits (bus), how much it can give (p_nom times p_max_pu, a time series for the wind farm, 1 for the gas plant) and what each MWh costs (marginal_cost). The load below is what they have to meet.
Output stays inside p_nom times p_max_pu
Wind output (area) follows its dashed upper bound whenever that is below the load, and the gas plant fills what is left; when the wind could give more than the load, the surplus is curtailed, since a Generator's output is a free variable between its bounds.
