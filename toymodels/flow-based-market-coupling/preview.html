NTC, flow-based and nodal market coupling

The same generators, loads and lines are cleared as a nodal market (the physics), as a zonal market with fixed bilateral exchange limits (NTC) and as a flow-based market whose only constraints on the zones' net positions are the lines' remaining margins mapped through the power transfer distribution factors (PTDF). The flow-based domain is a polygon in net-position space; the NTC box sits inside it, and the difference is exchange the NTC design leaves on the table.

One sale, every line: how a trade really flows
Zone A sells 100 MW to zone C. The power does not follow the deal: it splits over both paths by their impedance, two thirds on the direct A-C line and one third the long way through B, so the lines around B carry part of a trade B has nothing to do with. The shares are the power transfer distribution factors (PTDF).
Flow-based domain and the NTC box
Every point is a pair of zonal net positions (C balances the two). The polygon is what the lines can carry after the reliability margin; the box is what fixed bilateral limits allow. The flow-based clearing sits on the polygon's edge, the NTC clearing on the box's, and the nodal one just outside the polygon because it may use the full ratings.
Net positions by design
Cheap A exports, B and C import; the flow-based design lets A export more because it accounts for the loop flow through C instead of capping each border.
Dispatch cost by design
Nodal is the physical optimum; flow-based gets close; NTC pays for the margin its fixed limits waste.
