Cournot competition against a linear demand curve

Perfect competition is a PyPSA welfare maximisation (a quadratic programme, the demand curve entering as a sink with a quadratic marginal cost). The Cournot-Nash equilibrium is solved in closed form and confirmed by iterated best responses, each a small linopy quadratic programme; a single-firm monopoly closes the range. Withheld quantity, markup and deadweight loss follow.
