Static, dynamic and emergency line ratings

Two buses, cheap generation on one side, the load and a peaker on the other, one line between them over three hot days. The conductor carries less in the afternoon heat and more at night. Three cases: the conservative static rating (the afternoon value all day), the dynamic rating that follows the hour, and the dynamic rating with a two-hour emergency allowance at the evening peak.

Price spread across the line
Zero while the line has room; the peaker's cost minus the cheap unit's whenever it is full. The dynamic rating removes the spread at night, the emergency allowance at the peak.
Three-day cost and congestion rent by rating
A rating that follows the weather cuts both the dispatch cost and the rent the congestion transfers; the emergency allowance trims the last peak hours.
