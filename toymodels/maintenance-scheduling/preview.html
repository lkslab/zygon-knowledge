Maintenance scheduling

Three thermal units and two weeks of load with a weekend dip. The coal unit must be off for 72 consecutive hours somewhere in the fortnight; PyPSA's native maintenance attributes turn that into binary start variables and pick the window that costs least. The same outage forced into the first days shows what a badly timed one costs.
