Near-optimal alternatives (MGA)

The two-fortnight single-node expansion (wind, solar, gas, battery, 20 % CO2 cap) is solved to its optimum, then re-solved with the cost held within a slack of that optimum while each technology's capacity is pushed to its minimum and its maximum: modelling-to-generate-alternatives. The ranges show which capacities the cost optimum really pins down and which are a coin toss.

Capacity ranges within 5 % of the optimal cost
For each technology, the least and the most that can be built while total cost stays within the slack. A wide bar means the optimum barely constrains that choice.
The near-optimal mixes
Each row is one alternative: the mix that minimises or maximises one technology within the slack. Wind and solar trade against each other; gas capacity hardly moves because the cap and the dark weeks fix it.
Wind capacity range against cost slack
The feasible wind range opens quickly: a percent of extra cost buys a lot of freedom in the mix. That flatness of the cost surface is the point of MGA.
