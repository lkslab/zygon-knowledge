Modular and committable expansion

The two-fortnight single node with wind, solar, a battery and gas. Once: continuous capacities, no commitment (the linear relaxation). Then: gas capacity in whole blocks (`p_nom_mod`) and committable with a minimum part load and start-up costs, a mixed- integer problem. The lumpy plan costs noticeably more, and the zero-profit identity that holds for the LP breaks: with duals taken from a fixed-commitment re-solve, gas either over- or under-recovers its cost.

Capacity: continuous relaxation vs blocks with commitment
Gas snaps to a whole number of 100 MW blocks; wind, solar and the battery move to fill the gap the rounding leaves.
Dispatch in the winter fortnight with commitment
Committed gas never runs below 40 % of its blocks; the start-up cost makes it stay on through short dips rather than cycle.
Revenue against cost: the zero-profit identity breaks
In the LP every technology recovers its cost exactly. With blocks and commitment, prices come from a fixed-commitment re-solve and gas no longer breaks even: lumpy investment is why real markets need capacity mechanisms or make-whole payments.
