Myopic pathway with brownfield carry-over

Existing coal and gas start the story; each decade adds new wind, solar, gas and batteries at falling costs against a tightening CO2 cap and growing demand. Myopic means no horizon anticipates the next: capacity built in 2030 is inherited, not planned with 2050 in mind.

Installed capacity by horizon
Existing coal leaves at the end of its life; each horizon's wind, solar and battery additions are inherited by the next. Gas stays as firm capacity even when it barely runs.
Emissions against the cap
Emissions sit on the cap in every horizon: the constraint binds, and its shadow price is the CO2 price the horizon needs.
Annual cost by horizon
Capital cost of that horizon's new builds, the annuities still paid on inherited assets, and fuel. Demand grows across the horizons, so the total rises.
