Zonal market, redispatch and nodal pricing

Four buses: a windy, cheap north and a load-heavy south joined by two small lines. Three solves: (1) the nodal reference, (2) a copper-plate zonal market that clears as if the lines were infinite, (3) redispatch: the market dispatch is frozen and every unit gets an up-twin and a down-twin the operator can call at a cost, so the flows fit the real grid. The gap between (2)+(3) and (1) is the price of pretending.
