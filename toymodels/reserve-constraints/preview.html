Operating reserve as a custom constraint

One bus, a thermal fleet and wind over three days. Every hour the committed headroom must cover 10 % of load plus 20 % of what wind is producing. Reserve is a new variable per generator, capped by that generator's headroom; the dual of the requirement is the reserve price, and the extra dispatch cost is what holding it back costs.

Dispatch with the reserve held on top
Energy dispatch stacked in merit order; the top band is the headroom kept in reserve. To hold it, cheaper units run below capacity and the peaker fills in.
Reserve provision by unit
Who holds the reserve: the unit whose headroom is cheapest to leave idle, so the mid-merit units by day and the peaker when they are loaded.
Energy price with and without reserve, and the reserve price
The reserve requirement's dual is the reserve price: zero when headroom is free, positive in the hours where holding it forces a dearer unit into the energy stack.
