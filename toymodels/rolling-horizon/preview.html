Rolling horizon versus perfect foresight

The battery is where foresight shows: with the whole fortnight in view it saves charge for the expensive evenings; with a day at a time it fills and empties on what it can see. The cost gap between the two is the value of the forecast the rolling solve lacks.

Battery state of charge
Perfect foresight carries charge across days for the evenings that pay most; the rolling solves, seeing a day at a time, end every window empty and cycle on each day's own spread.
Hourly price
Where the two prices differ the rolling solve has a different unit at the margin, usually because the battery arrived at the evening peak empty or full at the wrong time.
Fortnight cost and battery revenue
Extra system cost over the perfect-foresight solve, and what the battery earned. The overlap does not close the gap here: every window still ends with the battery worth nothing, so it is emptied before the window closes whatever the next day looks like.
