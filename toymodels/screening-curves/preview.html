Screening curves and the optimal thermal mix

Each technology's annual cost per MW is a line in full-load hours: fixed cost plus marginal cost times hours. The cheapest line at every utilisation, read against the load duration curve, gives the optimal capacity of each technology. The same numbers come out of a PyPSA capacity expansion against that load duration curve.
