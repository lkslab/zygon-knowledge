Security-constrained optimal power flow (N-1)

A plain optimal power flow uses every line to its rating; if one line then trips, the loop pushes its flow onto the others and can overload them. The security-constrained solve adds, for every outage, the post-contingency flows as constraints, and holds spare capacity on the lines at a cost. Both dispatches are re-checked with a linear power flow under each outage to show what N-1 buys.
