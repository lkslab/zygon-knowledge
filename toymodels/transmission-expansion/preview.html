Transmission expansion between a windy and a load-rich zone

Two zones joined by one extendable bidirectional link. The north has cheap wind and little load; the south has the demand and a gas fleet. Sweeping the link's annualised cost per MW shows the trade-off between building wires, building gas in the south and curtailing wind in the north, and checks that at the optimum the link's congestion rent exactly pays for it.
