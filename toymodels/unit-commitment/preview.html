A plant is on or off, and on means at least its minimum
The CCGT over two days. Dispatched as if it could run at any level (dashed), it often idles at a few dozen MW. A real one runs at no less than 240 MW once on, and costs money to start: with commitment it either holds its minimum, or switches off and lets the peaker cover the small gaps.
Commitment status
Each band is one unit: raised while committed. Minimum up and down times keep the cheap coal unit on through the night trough rather than cycling it.
Dispatch
Committed units run at least at their minimum part load; the peaker fills the evening.
Prices: fixed vs linearised commitment
With commitment frozen the price is the marginal cost of the flexible unit, and can sit below every marginal cost when a unit is forced to its minimum. The linearised problem prices start-up costs into the hours that trigger them.
