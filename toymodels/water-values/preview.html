Water values for a seasonal reservoir

Solved over a stylised year with perfect foresight, the dual of the reservoir's energy balance is the marginal value of stored water in every snapshot. A rolling-horizon solve that ignores it drains the reservoir on the first expensive week; the same rolling solve with the water value charged as the cost of releasing water behaves almost like the perfect-foresight one. The pattern of PyPSA's water-value example on a StorageUnit.

Reservoir level over the year
Perfect foresight keeps water for the winter; the naive rolling solve spends it as soon as gas is on the margin and runs dry before the melt. Charging the water value as the cost of releasing brings the rolling solve close to the foresight path.
Water value against the price (perfect foresight)
The dual of the reservoir balance: what one more stored MWh would save. It sits near the gas cost most of the year, falls toward zero when spill looms in spring and rises when the reservoir is the marginal source. Axis clipped at 200 EUR/MWh.
