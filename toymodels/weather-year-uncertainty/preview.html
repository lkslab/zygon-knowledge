Weather-year uncertainty in investment

The two-fortnight single node with wind, solar, gas and a battery. Three weather scenarios differ in their wind and solar time series (a calm, a normal and a windy year), equally likely and unknown when capacities are chosen. Three deterministic plans, the two-stage stochastic plan, and the normal-year plan evaluated across all three years give the value of the stochastic solution and of perfect information, this time for weather rather than a fuel price.

Capacity by planning strategy
A plan for a calm year buys gas and solar, a plan for a windy year buys wind; the stochastic plan sits between and keeps a little more firm capacity than any single year asks for.
Realised annual cost under each weather year
The normal-year plan pays for its optimism in the calm year, mostly through gas and lost load; the stochastic plan gives up a little in the windy year for that.
Wind and solar curtailment under each weather year
Curtailment is the flip side of security: the plan that is safe in the calm year wastes more in the windy one.
